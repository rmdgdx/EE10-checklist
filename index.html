A high score does not guarantee publication or indexing.

    Priority revisions

      Suggested structure for the next revision

      Use this sequence to make the proposal easier for an engineering panel and future journal reviewers to evaluate:

      Problem and consequence → evidence-based research gap → measurable objective → proposed technical contribution → operationalized variables → reproducible design and benchmark → analysis matched to each objective → expected engineering significance, limitations, safety, and ethics.

      Decision guidance

      Basis of adaptation: Scopus Content Selection and Advisory Board dimensions for journal content include academic contribution, abstract clarity, conformity to aims and scope, readability, peer review, international relevance, and publication ethics.

      Official source: Scopus content policy and selection — Elsevier. The scoring model and thresholds on this page are educational adaptations, not official Scopus criteria for individual proposals.
